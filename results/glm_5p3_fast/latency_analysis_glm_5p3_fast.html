GLM 5.3 Fast · September 19, 2026

Sub-second median, with a recurring semantic miss.

Across three complete runs, GLM 5.3 Fast achieved 28/30 accuracy, a 0.72-second P50, and 27 of 30 requests below three seconds. The same Prague-region mistake appeared in Runs B and C, so the latency breakthrough does not yet satisfy the customer trust requirement.

Recommendation: GLM 5.3 Fast remains the leading latency candidate, but it is not safe as a standalone model. Add semantic validation for documented column meanings and retain a quality fallback before claiming the customer target is solved.

05 / Decision

Advance, then stress-test

GLM Fast is the first candidate to demonstrate the target combination in one complete run, but the repeated quality miss prevents an unconditional recommendation.

Add semantic checks

Validate geographic filters against documented column meaning and exact categorical values before execution.

Targets q_001 miss

Optimize year filtering

Prefer half-open ISO date ranges over per-row strftime when the schema supports index use.

Cuts q_007 local time

Run held-out repetitions

Use at least five runs over unseen paraphrases and schemas; report cold and warm latency separately.
